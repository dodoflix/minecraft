# CompassItem: useOn a lodestone plays the lock sound and binds a lodestone tracker; a single compass of a survival player is
# changed in place, a stack or a creative player's compass leaves the original and adds a lodestone compass (offhand too, or a
# dropped item when the inventory is full); any other block gives no result. inventoryTick clears the target of a tracker whose
