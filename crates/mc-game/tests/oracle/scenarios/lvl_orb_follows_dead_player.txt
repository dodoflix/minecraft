# An experience orb that follows a player keeps following the Player object after the player died and respawned: the dead object stays at the place
# of its death while the new player (another object) appears at the world spawn, 6 blocks from the place of death.
level void
command_env
difficulty peaceful
entity_counter 100
fill -12 99 -12 40 99 12 minecraft:stone
cmd setworldspawn -6 100 0
spawn_player Alice survival 0.5 100 0.5 0 0
tick 2
dump packets:Alice:none
level_seed 5
spawn orbA minecraft:experience_orb 4.5 100.5 0.5 {Value:3s}
watch xentities
tick 3
watch off
note Alice dies, the orb follows her on
hurt Alice minecraft:generic_kill 1000
watch xentities
tick 3
watch off
note Alice respawns at the world spawn
respawn Alice
watch xentities
tick 6
watch off
dump players
