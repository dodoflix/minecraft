# BlocksAttacks.disable: an axe hit on a blocking player puts the shield on cooldown, stops the use and plays the disable sound; the cooldown packet
# is sent in the same tick as the hit (ServerItemCooldowns.addCooldown), not on the next one. A sword only blocks (no cooldown).
level flat
preload 2
difficulty normal
watch events
level_seed 5
spawn_player V survival 0.5 -60 2.0 180 0
entity_seed V 101
give V offhand minecraft:shield
spawn_player A survival 0.5 -60 0.5 0 0
entity_seed A 102
give A mainhand minecraft:diamond_axe
spawn_player W survival 5.5 -60 2.0 180 0
entity_seed W 103
give W offhand minecraft:shield
spawn_player B survival 5.5 -60 0.5 0 0
entity_seed B 104
give B mainhand minecraft:iron_sword
tick 2
dump packets:V:none packets:W:none
note an axe hit disables the blocking shield at once
use V off
tick 40
dump use:V cooldowns:V packets:V:none
attack A V
dump use:V cooldowns:V events packets:V:cooldown,entity_event,hurt_animation,damage_event,sound
tick 1
dump use:V cooldowns:V events packets:V:cooldown,entity_event,hurt_animation,damage_event,sound
note a sword hit is blocked and the shield stays in use
use W off
tick 40
dump packets:W:none
attack B W
dump use:W cooldowns:W events packets:W:cooldown,entity_event,hurt_animation,damage_event,sound
tick 1
dump use:W cooldowns:W events packets:W:cooldown,entity_event,hurt_animation,damage_event,sound
