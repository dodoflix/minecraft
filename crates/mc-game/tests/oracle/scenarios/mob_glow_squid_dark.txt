# hurt squid flees (SquidFleeGoal) and a squid hurt by another squid keeps its ink behaviour; the saved data carries DarkTicksRemaining.

mobspawn Squid minecraft:squid 14.5 -57 2.5 0 0 {PersistenceRequired:1b}

hurt Glow minecraft:generic 1 by=Squid

hurt Squid minecraft:generic 1 by=Glow
