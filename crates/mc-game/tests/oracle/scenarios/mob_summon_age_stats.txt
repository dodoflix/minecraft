# A goat or hoglin loaded with a negative Age (/summon, /data merge) runs the age boundary while the data loads: the attack damage (and a
# hoglin's experience) of a calf replaces the attribute value that was just loaded, and merging the age back across zero restores the adult's.
command_env
level flat
preload 4
difficulty normal
entity_counter 14100
level_seed 14101
spawn_player Steve creative 0.5 -60 0.5 0 0
tick 2
note summoned calves: baby attack damage although the tag carries no attributes
cmd summon minecraft:goat 4.5 -60 4.5 {Age:-24000,NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b}
cmd summon minecraft:hoglin 8.5 -60 4.5 {Age:-24000,NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b,IsImmuneToZombification:1b}
cmd summon minecraft:goat 12.5 -60 4.5 {Age:-24000,NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b,attributes:[{id:"minecraft:attack_damage",base:7.0d}]}
cmd summon minecraft:goat 16.5 -60 4.5 {Age:0,NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b,attributes:[{id:"minecraft:attack_damage",base:7.0d}]}
dump mob:goat#1 mob:hoglin#1 mob:goat#2 mob:goat#3
note a calf merged to an adult and an adult merged to a calf
nbt goat#1 {Age:0}
nbt hoglin#1 {Age:0}
nbt goat#3 {Age:-24000}
dump mob:goat#1 mob:hoglin#1 mob:goat#3
note merging a negative age into a calf crosses nothing: the attack damage stays as it is
nbt goat#3 {attributes:[{id:"minecraft:attack_damage",base:5.0d}]}
nbt goat#3 {Age:-100}
dump mob:goat#3
