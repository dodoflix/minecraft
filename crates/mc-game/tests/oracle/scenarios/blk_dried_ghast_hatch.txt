# A dried ghast block that holds three levels of water hatches a ghastling on its scheduled tick (the `dispense` verb runs the block's tick): the block goes, the ghastling faces the way the
# block faced with its head turned to the same angle, and the spawn sound plays. A block with less water takes a level and plays the
# transition sound, a dry one gives a level back. The ghastlings then float on their brain: wander, and follow a player with a snowball.
level flat
difficulty normal
preload 3
entity_counter 1700
level_seed 1701
rand_value 99
spawn_player Steve creative 0.5 -60 0.5 0 0
block 4 -60 6 minecraft:dried_ghast[facing=north,hydration=3,waterlogged=true]
block 8 -60 6 minecraft:dried_ghast[facing=east,hydration=3,waterlogged=true]
block 12 -60 6 minecraft:dried_ghast[facing=south,hydration=3,waterlogged=true]
block 16 -60 6 minecraft:dried_ghast[facing=west,hydration=3,waterlogged=true]
block 20 -60 6 minecraft:dried_ghast[facing=east,hydration=1,waterlogged=true]
block 24 -60 6 minecraft:dried_ghast[facing=east,hydration=2,waterlogged=false]
tick 1
dump blocks:3,-60,5,25,-60,7 events entities
note a block with less than three levels of water takes one and plays the transition sound; a dry one dries a level
dispense 20 -60 6
dispense 24 -60 6
dump blocks:19,-60,5,25,-60,7 events entities
note each of the four facings hatches
level_seed 1702
dispense 4 -60 6
dump blocks:3,-60,5,25,-60,7 events entities mobs
dispense 8 -60 6
dump blocks:3,-60,5,25,-60,7 events entities mobs
dispense 12 -60 6
dump blocks:3,-60,5,25,-60,7 events entities mobs
dispense 16 -60 6
dump blocks:3,-60,5,25,-60,7 events entities mobs events
note the ghastlings float about on their brain
watch mobs
tick 40
watch off
dump mobs brain:happy_ghast#1 goals:happy_ghast#1 events
note a player with a snowball tempts them
give Steve mainhand minecraft:snowball 16
tp Steve 10.5 -60 6.5
watch mobs
tick 40
watch off
dump mobs brain:happy_ghast#2 events
