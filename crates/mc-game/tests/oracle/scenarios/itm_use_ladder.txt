# The use ladder of ServerPlayerGameMode.useItemOn / useItem: spectators, cooldowns, the creative count restore, the shift key suppressing the block use, the empty-hand
# fallback, the offhand, the inventory resync after an item use, and the ITEM_USED_ON_BLOCK trigger (both of its call sites).
# The inventory, the cooldowns and the packets of the player are dumped after every step: `container_set_slot` / `container_set_content` show the resync.

note a survival player places a block: the stack shrinks, the stat counts
