# Recipe unlocks: advancement rewards (the `tick` trigger of the crafting table, `inventory_changed` of the item recipes, the
# `recipe_unlocked` criterion that follows), `/recipe give|take`, the seen and settings packets of the recipe book.
command_env
level flat
preload 2
spawn_player A survival 0.5 -60 3.5 180 0
tick 2
dump packets:A:recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove,recipe_book_settings advancements:A recipes:A
note an oak log in the inventory unlocks the planks recipe
give A hotbar0 minecraft:oak_log 1
tick 1
dump packets:A:recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove,recipe_book_settings advancements:A recipes:A
note cobblestone unlocks the recipes made of it
give A hotbar1 minecraft:cobblestone 1
tick 1
dump packets:A:recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove,recipe_book_settings advancements:A recipes:A
note seeing a recipe clears its highlight, the book settings are stored
recipe_seen A minecraft:crafting_table
recipe_seen A minecraft:oak_planks
recipe_settings A crafting true true
recipe_settings A smoker true false
dump packets:A:recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove,recipe_book_settings recipes:A
note /recipe give: one known, one unknown, everything
cmd recipe give A minecraft:oak_planks
cmd recipe give A minecraft:stick
cmd recipe give A minecraft:furnace
dump packets:A:recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove,recipe_book_settings advancements:A recipes:A
cmd recipe give A *
dump packets:A:recipe_book_remove,recipe_book_settings advancements:A recipes:A
note /recipe take
cmd recipe take A minecraft:stick
cmd recipe take A minecraft:stick
cmd recipe take A *
dump packets:A:recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove,recipe_book_settings advancements:A recipes:A
note the item recipes are granted again by the next inventory change
give A hotbar2 minecraft:oak_log 1
tick 1
dump packets:A:recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove,recipe_book_settings advancements:A recipes:A
