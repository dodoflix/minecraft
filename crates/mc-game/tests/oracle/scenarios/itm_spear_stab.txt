# The STAB player action of a spear (PiercingWeapon.attack, Player.stabAttack): the entities along the view line are all hit
# (cow, armor stand, husk, a survival player; a creative player is invulnerable to it), knockback, durability, exhaustion, the
# attack and hit sounds, the swing and the attack strength reset. A stab before the attack strength is back is refused, the
# sharpness bonus follows the attack strength. Alice stands at z=0.5 looking along +z, tilted 10 degrees down so that the line also meets a cow; every target has NoAI and plenty of health.
level flat
preload 3
level_seed 5
difficulty normal
spawn_player Alice survival 0.5 -60 0.5 0 10
spawn_player Bob survival 8.5 -60 4.0 0 0
give Alice mainhand minecraft:iron_spear
tick 40
dump packets:Alice:none packets:Bob:none events
note stab at nothing: swing, attack sound, post piercing attack
stab Alice
dump events player:Alice inventory:Alice stats:Alice
note the next stab is refused while the attack strength is below the item's minimum charge
stab Alice
dump events player:Alice
tick 40
spawn Cow minecraft:cow 0.5 -60 3.0 180 0 {NoAI:1b}
entity_seed Cow 11
attribute Cow minecraft:max_health 1000
set Cow health 1000
spawn Stand minecraft:armor_stand 0.5 -60 4.0 180 0 {NoAI:1b}
entity_seed Stand 12

set Husk health 1000
tick 2
dump entities
note one stab pierces cow, armor stand and husk
stab Alice

tick 40
note a target closer than the minimum reach is missed, the others are still hit
