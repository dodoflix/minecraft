# Ender dragon parts: summoning a dragon takes eight more entity ids right after its own (head, neck, body, three tail parts, two wings),
# the parts exist as soon as it is added and sit at the world origin until the AI places them, a NoAI dragon never places them, only the
# dragon itself is sent to a watching player, a second dragon takes the next nine ids, and the parts are gone with their dragon.
command_env
level flat
preload 4
difficulty normal
level_seed 5001
entity_counter 5000
gamerule mob_drops false
spawn_player Steve creative 0.5 -60 0.5 0 0
tick 2
dump counter packets:Steve:none
note a NoAI dragon from /summon: nine ids, the parts at the origin, one add_entity and its data for the dragon only
cmd summon minecraft:ender_dragon 8.5 -60 8.5 {NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b}
dump counter entities
tick 1
dump counter parts:ender_dragon#1 xentity:ender_dragon#1 nbt:ender_dragon#1 packets:Steve:add_entity,set_entity_data
watch parts:ender_dragon#1
tick 3
watch off
note a second dragon from the entity creation path, its parts follow the counter
mobspawn D2 minecraft:ender_dragon -8.5 -60 8.5 90 0 {NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b}
entity_seed D2 7
tick 2
dump counter parts:D2 xentity:D2 edata:D2 packets:Steve:add_entity,set_entity_data
note moving the dragon does not move its parts
snap D2 -8.5 -50 8.5
tick 2
dump parts:D2 xentity:D2
note an entity created after the dragons takes the next id
cmd summon minecraft:armor_stand 0.5 -60 8.5
dump counter
note discarding a dragon removes its parts from the level
kill D2
tick 1
dump counter entities packets:Steve:remove_entities
