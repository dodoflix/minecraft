# dimension option: a block scenario in the nether. Lava schedules its fluid tick with the nether delay (FAST_LAVA) and the nether has no sky light.
dimension the_nether
preload 2
watch off
fill 0 100 0 4 104 4 minecraft:stone
block 2 102 2 minecraft:lava
dump blocks:2,102,2,2,102,2 ticks:0,100,0,4,104,4
tick 12
dump blocks:2,102,2,2,102,2 ticks:0,100,0,4,104,4
