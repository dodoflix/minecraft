# Ender dragon parts being hurt: the hitboxes of a NoAI dragon stay at the world origin, far from the dragon, and every attack on one of them is the
# dragon's hurt for that part. Direct hurts (a head and a neck hit counts in full, any other part a quarter plus at most one, nothing under 0.01, only
# a player or an always-hurts damage type reaches the health), a player's sword on a part (range from the part, the sword wears on the dragon), and
# arrows from a player that hit the head from above and a wing from the side; an arrow flies through the place of the parts once their dragon is gone.
command_env
level flat
preload 4
difficulty normal
level_seed 5101
entity_counter 5100
gamerule mob_drops false
spawn_player A survival 0.5 1 2.2 0 0
set A no_gravity true
give A mainhand minecraft:diamond_sword
tick 2
mobspawn D minecraft:ender_dragon 8.5 1 8.5 0 0 {NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b}
entity_seed D 11
partref Head D 0
partref Neck D 1
partref Body D 2
partref Tail D 3
partref Wing D 6
tick 2
dump packets:A:add_entity,set_entity_data
tick 23
dump packets:A:none parts:D xentity:D counter
note a hurt that is neither a player's nor of an always-hurts type changes nothing but is accepted
hurt Head generic 4
tick 1
dump xentity:D packets:A:damage_event,set_entity_data
note the head takes a player's hit in full
hurt Head player_attack 4 by=A
tick 25
dump xentity:D packets:A:damage_event,set_entity_data
note a wing takes a quarter plus one, the neck all of it, the body and a tail a quarter plus one
hurt Wing player_attack 4 by=A
tick 25
dump xentity:D
hurt Neck player_attack 4 by=A
tick 25
dump xentity:D
hurt Body player_attack 4 by=A
tick 25
dump xentity:D
hurt Tail player_attack 8 by=A
tick 25
dump xentity:D
note the dragon itself is its body
hurt D player_attack 4 by=A
tick 25
dump xentity:D
note under 0.01 after the scaling is refused, an always-hurts type needs no player, a second hit inside the invulnerable time is accepted too
hurt Wing player_attack 0.004 by=A
hurt Wing bad_respawn_point 20
hurt Head player_attack 3 by=A
tick 25
dump xentity:D
note the sword of a player standing inside the parts: the head in full, then a wing, and the sword wears on the dragon
attack A Head
tick 25
dump xentity:D inventory:A packets:A:damage_event
attack A Wing
tick 25
dump xentity:D inventory:A player:A
note an arrow falling from above hits the head, one flying in from the side the wing

kill D
