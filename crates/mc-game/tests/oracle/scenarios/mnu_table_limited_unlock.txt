# The crafting table with limited_crafting: a normal recipe the player does not know gives no result, a known one does and taking
# it counts the crafting statistic; a special recipe (decorated pot from four sherds) needs no knowledge and its take fires the
# recipe_crafted trigger (advancement adventure/craft_decorated_pot_using_only_sherds).
command_env
level flat
preload 2
spawn_player A survival 3.5 -60 7.5 0 0
block 3 -60 4 minecraft:crafting_table
give A hotbar0 minecraft:oak_planks 16
give A hotbar1 minecraft:arms_up_pottery_sherd 1
give A hotbar2 minecraft:angler_pottery_sherd 1
give A hotbar3 minecraft:archer_pottery_sherd 1
give A hotbar4 minecraft:blade_pottery_sherd 1
give A hotbar5 minecraft:bricks 4
tick 2
cmd recipe take A *
tick 1
dump packets:A:none recipes:A advancements:A stats:A
gamerule limited_crafting true
use_on A 3 -60 4 up main
note planks in two grid rows: the stick recipe is unknown, no result
click A 37 0 PICKUP
click A 1 1 PICKUP
click A 4 1 PICKUP
click A 37 0 PICKUP
dump menu:A packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,set_cursor_item,recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove recipes:A
note learn it: the next change of the grid shows the result, taking it counts and keeps the recipe known
cmd recipe give A minecraft:stick
click A 4 0 PICKUP
click A 4 0 PICKUP
dump menu:A packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,set_cursor_item,recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove recipes:A
level_seed 5
click A 0 0 PICKUP
dump menu:A packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,set_cursor_item,recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove recipes:A stats:A advancements:A
click A -999 0 PICKUP
note four sherds around the middle make a decorated pot even without the recipe book
click A 1 0 QUICK_MOVE
click A 4 0 QUICK_MOVE
click A 38 0 PICKUP
click A 2 1 PICKUP
click A 39 0 PICKUP
click A 4 1 PICKUP
click A 40 0 PICKUP
click A 6 1 PICKUP
click A 41 0 PICKUP
click A 8 1 PICKUP
dump menu:A packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,set_cursor_item,recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove recipes:A
click A 0 0 PICKUP
dump menu:A packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,set_cursor_item,recipe_book_add,recipe_book_remove recipes:A stats:A advancements:A inventory:A
