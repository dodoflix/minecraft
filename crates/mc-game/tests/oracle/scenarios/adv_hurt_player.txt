# The entity_hurt_player trigger (LivingEntity.hurtServer of a player), read by a data pack of damage conditions next to the vanilla deflect_arrow
# advancement: a zombie's bite, a projectile deflected by a shield and one that hits, fire and explosions without a source entity, the
