# thorns worn by a player in the nether that hurt a player of the overworld whose evoker fangs hit the wearer: the hurt is given the level of the wearer, so a shield
# that blocks it plays its sound there (the pitch drawn from that level's random), and the infested effect of the attacker spawns its silverfish there
# (at the attacker's coordinates, with the attacker's own random) while the attacker's own level hears nothing.
level void
command_env
end void
nether void
levels overworld,the_nether
preload 2
difficulty normal
entity_counter 100
fill -2 99 -2 2 99 12 minecraft:stone
in the_nether fill -2 99 -2 2 99 12 minecraft:netherrack
spawn_player Alice survival 0.5 100 0.5 -45 0
in the_nether spawn_player Bob survival 2.5 100 2.5 180 0
in the_nether give Bob chest minecraft:iron_chestplate[enchantments={"minecraft:thorns":10}]
in the_nether spawn_player Obs creative 1.5 100 1.5 0 0
give Alice offhand minecraft:shield
effect Alice minecraft:infested 0 6000
tick 2
dump packets:Alice:none player:Alice
in the_nether dump packets:Bob:none player:Bob packets:Obs:none
note Alice holds her shield up towards Bob, and her fangs hurt Bob: the thorns are blocked
use Alice off
tick 6
level_seed 5
in the_nether level_seed 11
in the_nether spawn F1 minecraft:evoker_fangs 2.5 100 2.5 0 0 {Warmup:0,Owner:[I;278005000,-707248403,-1814916367,-1817188393]}
in the_nether entity_seed F1 21
tick 10
dump player:Alice packets:Alice:entity_event,damage_event,hurt_animation,set_health,sound xentities
in the_nether dump player:Bob packets:Bob:entity_event,damage_event,hurt_animation,set_health packets:Obs:sound,add_entity xentities
note the shield is lowered and her fangs hurt Bob again: the thorns hurt her and her infested effect spawns silverfish in the nether
release Alice
tick 30
entity_seed Alice 29
in the_nether spawn F2 minecraft:evoker_fangs 2.5 100 2.5 0 0 {Warmup:0,Owner:[I;278005000,-707248403,-1814916367,-1817188393]}
in the_nether entity_seed F2 22
tick 10
dump player:Alice packets:Alice:entity_event,damage_event,hurt_animation,set_health xentities
in the_nether dump player:Bob packets:Bob:entity_event,damage_event,hurt_animation,set_health packets:Obs:sound,add_entity xentities
tick 3
dump xentities
in the_nether dump xentities
