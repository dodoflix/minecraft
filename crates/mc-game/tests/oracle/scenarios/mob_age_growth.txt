# AgeableMob: a baby grows one tick at a time and is an adult at age 0; wheat feeds a baby up (a tenth of the remaining seconds, forced
# age with happy villager particles every 4th tick for 40 ticks); an adult counts its positive age (breeding cooldown) down; feeding an
# adult starts love; a golden dandelion locks and unlocks a baby's growth (pause/reset particles).
level flat
preload 2
difficulty normal
time 6000
entity_counter 300
spawn_player Steve survival 0.5 -60 0.5 0 0
give Steve mainhand minecraft:wheat 16
mobspawn B1 minecraft:cow 3.5 -60 0.5 0 0 {Age:-60,NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b}
mobspawn B2 minecraft:cow 3.5 -60 3.5 0 0 {Age:-24000,NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b}
mobspawn A1 minecraft:cow 6.5 -60 0.5 0 0 {Age:50,NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b}
mobspawn A2 minecraft:cow 6.5 -60 3.5 0 0 {NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b}
mobspawn B3 minecraft:sheep 9.5 -60 0.5 0 0 {Age:-24000,NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b}
entity_seed B1 5
entity_seed B2 6
entity_seed A1 7
entity_seed A2 8
entity_seed B3 9
tick 2
dump mob:B1 mob:B2 mob:A1 mob:A2 mob:B3
note a baby becomes an adult when its age reaches 0, an adult counts its cooldown down to 0
watch mob:B1 mob:A1 packets:Steve:level_particles
tick 62
watch off
dump mob:B1 mob:B2 mob:A1 mob:A2 mob:B3
note wheat on a baby: 24000 ticks = 1200 s, a tenth is 120 s = 2400 ticks of growth, forced age and particles
interact Steve B2
dump mob:B2 packets:Steve:level_particles,entity_event
watch mob:B2 packets:Steve:level_particles
tick 45
watch off
note feeding the baby again while the forced age timer ran out
interact Steve B2
tick 2
dump mob:B2 nbt:B2 packets:Steve:level_particles,entity_event
note feeding an adult puts it in love, feeding a cooling-down adult does nothing
interact Steve A2
tick 2
dump mob:A2 packets:Steve:level_particles,entity_event
interact Steve A2
nbt A1 {Age:100}
interact Steve A1
tick 2
dump mob:A1 mob:A2 packets:Steve:level_particles,entity_event
note forced age of a nearly grown baby: ageUp passes 0, the forced age becomes the age
nbt B2 {Age:-30,ForcedAge:-2000}
interact Steve B2
tick 2
dump mob:B2 nbt:B2
note golden dandelion: lock (age reset to a baby, persistent, pause particles), growth stops, unlock (reset particles)
give Steve mainhand minecraft:golden_dandelion 4
interact Steve B3
dump mob:B3 nbt:B3 packets:Steve:level_particles
watch mob:B3 packets:Steve:level_particles
tick 45
watch off
interact Steve B3
tick 2
dump mob:B3 nbt:B3 packets:Steve:level_particles
note an adult ignores the dandelion
interact Steve A2
tick 2
dump mob:A2 packets:Steve:level_particles
note age and forced age survive the NBT round trip
nbt B1 {Age:-77,ForcedAge:-5}
tick 3
dump mob:B1 nbt:B1
