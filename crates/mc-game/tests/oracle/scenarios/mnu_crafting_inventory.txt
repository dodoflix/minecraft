# The 2x2 crafting grid of the player's own menu through the click packets: every mutation of the grid (set, remove, the take
# that shrinks every ingredient) recomputes the result and sends one container_set_slot of slot 0, so the intermediate results
# are part of the comparison. Slots: 0 result, 1-4 grid, 9-35 inventory, 36-44 hotbar. Player A has limited_crafting off and its
# recipe book is not compared (the unlock chain is); player B is the limited_crafting case and starts with an empty inventory so
# that no advancement unlocks a recipe behind the scenario's back.
