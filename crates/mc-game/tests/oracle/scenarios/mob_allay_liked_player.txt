# Allay liked player: the player an allay was given an item by cannot hurt it and counts as an ally, everyone else can (hurt
# panics it, it heals one health point every 10 ticks); the allay stays near the liked player and the brain memories are saved;
# a leash does not make it stay close to its holder.
level flat
preload 2
difficulty normal
time 6000
entity_counter 1000
spawn_player A survival 0.5 -60 0.5 0 0
spawn_player B survival 0.5 -60 6.5 0 0
mobspawn al minecraft:allay 4.5 -59 0.5 0 0 {PersistenceRequired:1b}

entity_seed al 101

give A mainhand minecraft:iron_ingot 1
hurt al player_attack 2 by=A

tick 30
dump mob:al brain:al

interact A al main
tick 1
dump mob:al brain:al events
note hits by the liked player do nothing, hits by B and other damage hurt
hurt al player_attack 3 by=A
tick 1
dump mob:al events
hurt al player_attack 3 by=B
tick 1
dump mob:al brain:al events
tick 25
dump mob:al brain:al events
hurt al magic 1
tick 1
dump mob:al events

tp A 24.5 -60 0.5
tick 40
dump mob:al brain:al

dump mob:al brain:al
