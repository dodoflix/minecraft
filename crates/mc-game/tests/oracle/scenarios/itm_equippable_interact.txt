# Equippable.equipOnTarget through Player.interactOn / ItemStack.interactLivingEntity and the mobInteract of the mobs that call it
# (Pig, Strider, AbstractHorse, Wolf): saddles, horse armor and wolf armor on the allowed and not allowed entity types, babies,
# owned and wild animals. A successful equip splits one item off, plays the equip sound and marks the slot as a guaranteed drop (drop_chances in
# the nbt dump); creative keeps the stack.
