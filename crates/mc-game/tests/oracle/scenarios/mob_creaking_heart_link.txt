# A creaking heart at night spawns its creaking within a few ticks of the 20..24 tick ticker; the creaking is invulnerable to anything
# but a player or a mob while the heart is its protector, a player's hit relays to the heart (particles, resin clumps, sounds, the
# comparator) and the heart is its home. The link ends when the day comes, when a player is stuck inside the creaking, and when the
# creaking is farther than 34 blocks from the heart.
level flat
preload 4
entity_counter 4400
level_seed 4401
difficulty normal
gamerule mob_drops false
time 15000
spawn_player P survival 6.5 -60 6.5 0 0
tick 30
# nothing spawns naturally on a glass floor; the heart needs both rules
fill -80 -61 -80 95 -61 95 minecraft:glass
gamerule spawn_mobs true
gamerule spawn_monsters true
level_seed 4400
attribute P minecraft:max_health 2000
set P health 2000
fill -1 -61 -1 1 -57 1 minecraft:pale_oak_log[axis=y]
block 0 -60 0 minecraft:creaking_heart[axis=y,creaking_heart_state=dormant,natural=true]
block 2 -60 0 minecraft:comparator[facing=west,mode=compare,powered=false]
tick 1
dump blocks:-1,-61,-1,3,-57,1 be:0,-60,0 events
note night: the heart wakes and spawns the creaking
watch be:0,-60,0 xentities events packets:P:level_particles,entity_event,sound_entity,block_update,block_entity_data
tick 80
watch off
dump mobs blocks:-1,-61,-1,3,-57,1 be:0,-60,0 nbt:creaking#1 events
note a player hits the creaking: the heart emits particles and resin, a second hit within the invulnerable window does nothing
hurt creaking#1 minecraft:player_attack 1 by=P direct=P
watch be:0,-60,0 mobs events packets:P:level_particles,entity_event,sound_entity,block_update
tick 3
hurt creaking#1 minecraft:player_attack 1 by=P direct=P
tick 30
watch off
note a hit by a mob relays no particles, a hit with no player or mob behind it does nothing, hits after the heart's particles ran out
tick 100
hurt creaking#1 minecraft:mob_attack 1 by=P direct=P
tick 10
hurt creaking#1 minecraft:fall 1
tick 10
hurt creaking#1 minecraft:player_attack 1 by=P direct=P
tick 3
dump mobs blocks:-1,-61,-1,3,-57,1 be:0,-60,0 events
note the day ends the link: the creaking crumbles
time 1000
watch mobs be:0,-60,0 events packets:P:level_particles,entity_event,sound_entity
tick 40
watch off
dump mobs blocks:-1,-61,-1,3,-57,1 be:0,-60,0 events
note night again: a new creaking is moved into a one block cell with the player; five heart updates with the player's eyes inside it end the link
fill 9 -60 9 11 -57 11 minecraft:stone
fill 10 -60 10 10 -58 10 minecraft:air
time 15000
tick 60
dump mobs be:0,-60,0
tp P 10.5 -60 10.5 0 0
tp creaking#2 10.5 -60 10.5
watch mobs be:0,-60,0 events
tick 130
watch off
dump mobs be:0,-60,0 events
note a creaking pushed beyond 34 blocks from the heart ends the link
tick 60
dump mobs be:0,-60,0 events
nbt creaking#3 {NoAI:1b}
tp creaking#3 40.5 -60 40.5
tick 40
dump mobs be:0,-60,0 events
