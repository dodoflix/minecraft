# The portal delay rules: a survival player must stand in a nether portal for players_nether_portal_default_delay ticks, a creative one for
# players_nether_portal_creative_delay; once the delay is over the portal cooldown starts and allow_entering_nether_using_portals false
# keeps the player where it is (this level has no nether to arrive in). Dumped after every tick: the portal cooldown and the ticks spent
# in the portal. A third portal runs with a delay of 0.

gamerule players_nether_portal_default_delay 6
gamerule players_nether_portal_creative_delay 2
