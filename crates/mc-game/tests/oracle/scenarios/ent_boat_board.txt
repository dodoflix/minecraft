# A boat that is held under water (here by a ceiling) throws its passengers off after 60 ticks and cannot be boarded while it is out of
# control; the time restarts from zero when the boat comes up. Passengers: a pig and a player (who leaves through the dismount search).
level flat
preload 4
level_seed 331
entity_counter 3500
spawn_player O survival 20.5 -60 8.5 0 0
spawn_player P survival 8.5 -60 3.5 0 0
fill 0 -63 0 4 -59 4 minecraft:stone
fill 1 -62 1 3 -60 3 minecraft:water
spawn B minecraft:oak_boat 2.5 -61.5 2.5 0 0
entity_seed B 341
mobspawn pig minecraft:pig 8.5 -60 6.5 0 0 nofinalize {PersistenceRequired:1b,NoAI:1b,Silent:1b}
entity_seed pig 342
tick 3
dump packets:O:none packets:P:none
note the rider boards while the boat is in the pool
ride pig B
ride P B
accept_teleport P
tick 5
dump xentity:B player:P mob:pig events packets:P:set_passengers,teleport_entity,player_position packets:O:set_passengers,teleport_entity
tick 30
dump xentity:B player:P mob:pig
tick 24
dump xentity:B player:P mob:pig
note after 60 ticks under water everyone is thrown off
tick 2
dump xentity:B player:P mob:pig events packets:P:set_passengers,teleport_entity,player_position,set_entity_data packets:O:set_passengers,teleport_entity,move_entity_pos
note a boat that is out of control cannot be boarded
interact P B main
tick 2
dump xentity:B player:P events packets:P:set_passengers,teleport_entity,player_position
note the pool is drained: the boat falls to the floor, the counter restarts at zero

accept_teleport P
tick 2
