# Leaving the End through the exit portal after the credits: the player arrives where its respawn point says (a bed, a charged respawn anchor in
# the nether, a forced point, a point whose block is gone, none at all: the world spawn with a message for the missing block); items, mobs and
# arrows go to the world spawn of the overworld (adjusted for their size). The respawn config of a player is merged in with `nbt`.
