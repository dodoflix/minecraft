# BrushItem: useOn starts the brush animation only while the view ray hits a block (CONSUME); every 10 ticks (elapsed % 10 == 5) the
# brush plays the block's brush sound (generic for an ordinary block) and brushes a suspicious block; the brush loses one durability
# when a block is brushed away, from the hand that holds it; looking at nothing releases the item. Players aim by their spawn yaw
# and pitch (pitch 15 from the feet hits the north face of the block 4 ahead), pitch -90 looks at the sky.
level flat
preload 3
watch events
level_seed 5
spawn_player A survival 4.5 -60 0.5 0 15
spawn_player C creative 20.5 -60 0.5 0 15
give A mainhand minecraft:brush
give C mainhand minecraft:brush
block 4 -60 4 minecraft:suspicious_sand
be_nbt 4 -60 4 {LootTable:"minecraft:archaeology/desert_pyramid",LootTableSeed:12345L}
tick 3
note main hand: ten strokes brush the block away (sounds at elapsed 5, 15, ..., loot dropped north, brush damaged by one), the later strokes hit plain sand
use_on A 4 -60 4 north main
tick 20
dump blocks:4,-60,4,4,-60,4 be:4,-60,4 inventory:A events
tick 40
dump blocks:4,-60,4,4,-60,4 be:4,-60,4 inventory:A events
tick 50
dump blocks:4,-60,4,4,-60,4 be:4,-60,4 inventory:A entities events
note looking at the sky releases the brush
snap A 4.5 -60 0.5 0 -90
tick 12
dump inventory:A events
note a stored item, looking away mid-brush keeps the progress until the reset
block 8 -60 4 minecraft:suspicious_gravel
be_nbt 8 -60 4 {item:{id:"minecraft:diamond",count:3}}
snap A 8.5 -60 0.5 0 15
tick 2
use_on A 8 -60 4 north main
tick 30
dump blocks:8,-60,4,8,-60,4 be:8,-60,4 inventory:A events
snap A 8.5 -60 0.5 0 -90
tick 12
dump blocks:8,-60,4,8,-60,4 be:8,-60,4 inventory:A events
snap A 8.5 -60 0.5 0 15
tick 2
use_on A 8 -60 4 north main
tick 60
dump blocks:8,-60,4,8,-60,4 be:8,-60,4 inventory:A events
tick 40
dump blocks:8,-60,4,8,-60,4 be:8,-60,4 inventory:A entities events
snap A 8.5 -60 0.5 0 -90
tick 12
note an ordinary block: the generic sound only, no durability change
block 12 -60 4 minecraft:stone
snap A 12.5 -60 0.5 0 15
tick 2
use_on A 12 -60 4 north main
tick 35
dump blocks:12,-60,4,12,-60,4 inventory:A events
snap A 12.5 -60 0.5 0 -90
tick 12
note off hand: the durability is taken from the off hand brush
give A mainhand minecraft:stick
give A offhand minecraft:brush
block 16 -60 4 minecraft:suspicious_sand
be_nbt 16 -60 4 {item:{id:"minecraft:gold_ingot",count:2}}
snap A 16.5 -60 0.5 0 15
tick 2
use_on A 16 -60 4 north off
tick 110
dump blocks:16,-60,4,16,-60,4 be:16,-60,4 inventory:A entities events
snap A 16.5 -60 0.5 0 -90
tick 12
note creative: the block is brushed away and the brush keeps its durability
block 20 -60 4 minecraft:suspicious_sand
be_nbt 20 -60 4 {item:{id:"minecraft:emerald",count:2}}
tick 2
use_on C 20 -60 4 north main
tick 110
dump blocks:20,-60,4,20,-60,4 be:20,-60,4 inventory:C entities events
snap C 20.5 -60 0.5 0 -90
tick 12
note the last point of durability: the brush breaks when the block is brushed away
give A mainhand minecraft:brush[damage=63]
block 24 -60 4 minecraft:suspicious_gravel
be_nbt 24 -60 4 {item:{id:"minecraft:iron_ingot",count:2}}
snap A 24.5 -60 0.5 0 15
tick 2
use_on A 24 -60 4 north main
tick 110
dump blocks:24,-60,4,24,-60,4 be:24,-60,4 inventory:A entities events
