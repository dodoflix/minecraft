# An experience orb keeps the Player object it follows (ExperienceOrb.followingPlayer) while that player is within 8 blocks of it, also after the
# player left the orb's level (the object keeps its coordinates there) or died and respawned (the dead object stays where it died): the orb flies
# on toward those coordinates until they are too far away or the player is a spectator.
level void
command_env
end void
nether void
levels overworld,the_nether,the_end
preload 2
difficulty peaceful
entity_counter 100
fill -12 99 -12 12 99 12 minecraft:stone
in the_nether fill -12 99 -12 12 99 12 minecraft:netherrack
cmd setworldspawn 0 100 0
spawn_player Alice survival 0.5 100 0.5 0 0
spawn_player Bob survival -0.5 100 -0.5 0 0
tick 2
dump packets:Alice:none packets:Bob:none
level_seed 5
note two orbs near Alice and Bob that follow them
spawn orbA minecraft:experience_orb 4.5 100.5 0.5 {Value:3s}
spawn orbB minecraft:experience_orb -4.5 100.5 0.5 {Value:3s}
watch xentities
tick 3
watch off
note Alice goes to the nether, to the same coordinates: the orb keeps following her
tp_dim Alice the_nether 0.5 100 0.5
watch xentities
tick 5
watch off
note Alice moves in the nether, 4 blocks away from the first orb
in the_nether tp Alice 4.5 100 4.5
watch xentities
tick 8
watch off
