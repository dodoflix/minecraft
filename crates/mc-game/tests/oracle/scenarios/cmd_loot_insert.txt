# `loot insert`: the items go into the first slot that accepts them (a free slot, or a stack of the same item): chests, barrels, hoppers,
# furnaces, brewing stands, shulker boxes, decorated pots, jukeboxes, lecterns, bookshelves, crafters, a double chest, a full container,
# targets that are no container, and every loot source as the origin of the items.
