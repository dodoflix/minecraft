# ServerPlayer.enderPearls with a pearl in another level than its owner: Alice (overworld) has a pearl hovering in the nether (the pearl was moved there with
# `tp_dim`). Her saved data lists the pearl with `ender_pearl_dimension` of the nether, not of her own level. When she leaves, PlayerList.remove removes the
# pearl of the nether with her; when she joins again with her saved data (`load`), loadAndSpawnEnderPearls puts the saved pearl back into the nether and places
# its ticket there.
level void
command_env
end void
nether void
levels overworld,the_nether,the_end
preload 2
difficulty peaceful
entity_counter 100
fill -2 99 -2 2 99 2 minecraft:stone
in the_nether fill -2 99 -2 2 99 2 minecraft:netherrack
spawn_player Alice survival 0.5 100 0.5 0 -90
give Alice mainhand minecraft:ender_pearl 3
tick 2
level_seed 5
use Alice
tick 1
tp_dim ender_pearl#1 the_nether 0.5 150 0.5
set ender_pearl#1 no_gravity true
tick 3
note the saved data lists the pearl with the level it is in
dump nbt:Alice
in the_nether dump entities nbt:ender_pearl#1
note Alice leaves: the pearl of the nether goes with her
leave Alice
tick 2
dump entities levels players
in the_nether dump entities levels
note Alice joins again with her saved data: the saved pearl is spawned into the nether
spawn_player Alice survival 0.5 100 0.5 0 -90 load
tick 2
dump levels players nbt:Alice
in the_nether dump entities levels nbt:ender_pearl#2
tick 45
dump nbt:Alice
in the_nether dump entities nbt:ender_pearl#2
