# The brewing stand menu through the player's listener and the server's own tick order: the menu sees the fuel, the brewing progress and
# the finished potions only after the stand ticked (block entities tick after the player), quick move into the three slot kinds, the
# interact_with_brewingstand statistic, and what the stand keeps and drops when its block is broken.
command_env
level flat
preload 2
spawn_player A survival 0.5 -60 3.5 180 0
block 0 -60 0 minecraft:brewing_stand
give A hotbar0 minecraft:potion[potion_contents={potion:"minecraft:water"}] 1
give A hotbar1 minecraft:potion[potion_contents={potion:"minecraft:water"}] 1
give A hotbar2 minecraft:potion[potion_contents={potion:"minecraft:awkward"}] 1
give A hotbar3 minecraft:nether_wart 3
give A hotbar4 minecraft:blaze_powder 3
give A hotbar5 minecraft:glistering_melon_slice 2
give A hotbar6 minecraft:dirt 4
tick 2
dump packets:A:none stats:A be:0,-60,0
use_on A 0 -60 0 up main
dump menu:A stats:A packets:A:open_screen,container_set_content,container_set_data
note quick move: potions into the bottle slots, wart as the ingredient, blaze powder as the fuel, dirt nowhere but the other inventory half
click A 32 0 QUICK_MOVE
click A 33 0 QUICK_MOVE
click A 34 0 QUICK_MOVE
click A 38 0 QUICK_MOVE
click A 35 0 QUICK_MOVE
click A 36 0 QUICK_MOVE
dump menu:A blocks:0,-60,0,0,-60,0 be:0,-60,0 packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,container_set_data,set_cursor_item
note the fuel is burnt by the first stand tick, the menu learns about it with the next player tick
tick 1
dump menu:A blocks:0,-60,0,0,-60,0 be:0,-60,0 packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,container_set_data,set_cursor_item
tick 1
dump menu:A blocks:0,-60,0,0,-60,0 be:0,-60,0 packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,container_set_data,set_cursor_item
tick 200
dump menu:A events be:0,-60,0 packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,container_set_data,set_cursor_item
tick 201
dump menu:A events blocks:0,-60,0,0,-60,0 be:0,-60,0 packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,container_set_data,set_cursor_item
note taking a brewed potion out of its slot awards the brew_potion advancement
click A 0 0 PICKUP
dump menu:A advancements:A packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,container_set_data,set_cursor_item
click A 0 0 PICKUP
note a second brew: the melon slice as the ingredient
click A 37 0 PICKUP
click A 3 0 PICKUP
dump menu:A packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,container_set_data,set_cursor_item
tick 401
dump menu:A events be:0,-60,0 packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,container_set_data,set_cursor_item
tick 1
dump menu:A be:0,-60,0 packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,container_set_data,set_cursor_item
note breaking the block closes the menu and drops the stand's items
destroy 0 -60 0
tick 2
dump menu:A inventory:A packets:A:container_set_slot,container_set_content,container_set_data,container_close entities
